Privacy and data use

Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Iboti Calendar is operated by Ido Kahlon as a private calendar assistant for its owner and invited collaborators. This policy explains how the integration handles Google user data.

Information accessed

Iboti accesses Google Calendar event information needed to check availability and fulfill scheduling requests. This can include event titles, dates, times, descriptions, locations, attendee email addresses, and conferencing information. It also uses basic Google account-email information to verify that the intended automation account has signed in.

The integration requests Calendar event permissions and basic identity verification. It does not request Gmail, Drive, or Contacts access. The service exposes only the calendar specifically selected by the owner. Google account passwords are not collected by this integration.

How information is used

Information is used to answer calendar questions, find availability, propose meetings, and carry out authorized event creation, updates, cancellations, and invitations. The owner’s approval rules apply to calendar changes, including explicitly configured exceptions for selected groups.

Calendar data is not sold or used for advertising. Google Workspace API data is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. AI processing is used to provide the requested calendar-assistant features.

Sharing and processing

Relevant request and event information may be processed by the assistant’s configured AI and messaging providers to provide the requested features. Event invitations and notifications are sent through Google Calendar when authorized. People participating in an authorized conversation can see information shared in that conversation, and invited attendees receive the relevant invitation details.

Service providers’ handling of information is also subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies. The integration does not intentionally share calendar information for unrelated purposes.

Storage and protection

Google OAuth client credentials and refresh tokens are stored in the owner’s encrypted HashiCorp Vault. The Calendar service handles these credentials rather than including them in assistant replies.

Meeting-request history, assistant conversation history, and operational backups may retain scheduling information on the owner’s systems. Not all historical records are encrypted at rest. Access is limited through server and application permissions; these measures do not make a compromised administrator account safe.

Retention, deletion, and revocation

Scheduling information may remain in local request history, conversation history, and backups until the owner removes it. Deleting a Google Calendar event does not automatically delete historical chat or request records. Backup retention and removal may require separate handling.

The owner can revoke the integration’s access in Google Account connections or remove the automation account’s access in the calendar’s sharing settings. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not automatically erase previously retained records.

For removal of locally retained credentials or scheduling records, contact idokahlon96@gmail.com.

Google API user data

Iboti Calendar’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact and updates

For questions about this policy or your data, email idokahlon96@gmail.com. Material changes to the integration’s data practices will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
