Iboti Calendar helps its owner and invited collaborators check availability and schedule meetings through authorized assistant conversations.

It is a personal integration, not a public scheduling service. This website explains how the integration works and how it handles Google user data.

01

Check availability

Find available times on the calendar the owner has chosen to share with the automation account.

02

Schedule meetings

Create calendar events and send requested invitations, or update and cancel existing events.

03

Keep the owner in control

Calendar changes follow the owner’s approval rules. The owner can separately authorize creation requests from selected groups.

Why Google access is requested

Calendar event access lets Iboti read availability and create or change requested events. Basic account-email verification checks that the intended automation account is connected. This integration does not request access to Gmail messages, Google Drive files, or Google Contacts.

The Calendar service targets the owner’s specifically shared calendar. OAuth credentials are stored in the owner’s HashiCorp Vault and are not included in assistant replies.

Use and support

Use is limited to the owner and people the owner authorizes. Review meeting details before approval, and verify important appointments in Google Calendar. Access can be withdrawn at any time through Google Account permissions or the calendar’s sharing settings.

Iboti Calendar is an independent personal integration and is not a Google product. For access, privacy, or deletion questions, contact idokahlon96@gmail.com.
